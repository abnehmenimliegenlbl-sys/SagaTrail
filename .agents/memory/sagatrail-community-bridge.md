---
name: SagaTrail community bridge
description: Product principle for connecting existing Facebook hiking groups with SagaTrail communities.
---

SagaTrail should complement, not replace, existing Facebook hiking groups:
Facebook supplies reach and discovery; SagaTrail provides the opted-in
community space, route archive, structured meetups, participant coordination,
and safety information.

**Why:** Many large hiking groups mainly share photos and inspiration. A
frictionless admin-posted invite link is a better entry point than a forced
migration, a broad directory, or unreliable Facebook membership
synchronization.

**How to apply:** Keep group discovery link-led and user-controlled. Separate
external Facebook audience size from actual SagaTrail membership. Do not
scrape private groups, import members automatically, or make the community
dependent on Facebook APIs.
