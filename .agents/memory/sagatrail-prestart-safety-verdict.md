---
name: Pre-start safety verdict provenance
description: Priority rules for the compact route-start safety judgment.
---

The pre-start card must combine signals into one conservative verdict without claiming an official route closure from weak evidence. Thunderstorms, avalanche danger level 4–5, or a fresh community report marked blocked justify postponing the start; avalanche level 3, difficult weather/ground, or a canton-scoped warning justify caution. RSS notices without route geometry remain non-authoritative context.

**Why:** Weather is a point heuristic, EAWS is regional, community reports are unverified, and closure feeds are often canton-wide. Treating any one as a route-specific clearance or closure creates unsafe certainty.

**How to apply:** Keep source and uncertainty visible in the compact card. Only add a hard-closure state when a future authoritative, route-specific source exists; otherwise use “caution” or “postpone” and link to the source where possible. Keep time-dependent evaluation pure by passing the reference time in tests.
