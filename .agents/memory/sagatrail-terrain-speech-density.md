---
name: Terrain speech density
description: Product rule for keeping slope narration useful on winding routes
---

Detailed 50 m slope coloring must not become one spoken cue per color change. Dense nearby terrain sections should be thinned for narration, while the strongest section in the local window and very steep safety sections take priority.

**Why:** On winding routes, frequent short changes between incline directions make the audio distracting even when the map visualization is useful.

**How to apply:** Keep the map segmentation detailed, apply a separate minimum spacing to terrain voice sections, and never let normal sections displace a nearby very steep warning.
