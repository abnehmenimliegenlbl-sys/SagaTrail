---
name: Garmin Horizon UI
description: The approved visual and information hierarchy for SagaTrail's round Garmin Connect IQ screens.
---

Use the Horizon direction for Garmin: design within the round safe area, keep direction and maneuver distance dominant, place secondary data above the narrow lower arc, and use a small SagaTrail mountain/star mark in the header. Direction graphics are only simple arrows for left, right, straight ahead, and turn around. Avoid generic dashboard cards.

**Why:** The user rejected the card-based Garmin design as visually poor, then explicitly selected Horizon after comparing three distinct concepts. They also required every value to remain readable inside the circular display.

**How to apply:** Preserve the ridge/horizon composition and `#CC0000`/black/white/neutral/GPS-green palette when extending Garmin pages. Treat circle-safe placement and plain direction arrows as fixed requirements, not optional styling.
