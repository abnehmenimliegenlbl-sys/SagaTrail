---
name: AR route visibility across phone tilt
description: Ground-only route overlays disappear from an upright camera view; keep a restrained forward direction cue in addition to the anchored route.
---

The AR route needs two visual layers: a clearly visible ground-anchored trace for spatial accuracy and a small forward direction cue placed away from the camera and slightly above the ground plane.

**Why:** A physically correct route at roughly 1.25 m below the Viro origin is mostly outside an upright phone's camera view, while a billboard arrow at the user's feet becomes oversized when the phone points down.

**How to apply:** Keep the route line anchored and clearly visible, place the direction cue around 1–2 m ahead with restrained dimensions, and verify both upright and downward phone angles on a physical device.
