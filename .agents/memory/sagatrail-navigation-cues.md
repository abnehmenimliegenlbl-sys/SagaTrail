# Navigation cues alongside live saga narration

Turn-by-turn cues (e.g. "an der naechsten Weggabelung links") are computed
purely from real route geometry, never invented, and never generated by the
AI story pipeline. They are spoken as separate navigation announcements and
must never be appended to a saga chapter or sent as part of saga TTS.

- Detection (`artifacts/mobile/lib/navigationCues.ts`) walks `route.geometry`,
  computes bearing at >=60m segments (filters GPS/OSM node noise), and flags
  a turn as a cue when the bearing change is >=35 degrees. The first/last 5%
  of the route is excluded (arrival/departure narration shouldn't be
  interrupted by a turn cue), and cues are thinned to an even spread if more
  are found than requested.

**Why:** chapters can come from local deterministic content, server-generated
content, or a downloaded/cached copy. Appending route-specific text to any of
those chapters makes it sound like part of the legend and can create
route-specific TTS cache entries. Keeping navigation separate also preserves
the rule that an active turn announcement may coordinate with, but does not
mutate, story playback.

**How to apply:** keep `detectNavigationCues()` and its live approach/turn
flow for standalone navigation audio and watch notifications. Do not call a
story-text weaving helper, add `navCue()` output to chapter text, or include
route-specific directions in offline or online saga narration.
