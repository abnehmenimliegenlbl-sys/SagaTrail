The saga pointer is a dedicated compass-needle icon with a gold star above a mountain. The complete icon rotates with the live saga bearing so the star remains at the outward tip of the radial pointer. Do not counter-rotate it and do not substitute the general SparkMountain brand mark.

**Why:** The general SparkMountain mark is a brand element, while the attached compass artwork communicates directional orientation through its star tip.

**How to apply:** Keep the pointer artwork as a transparent image layer over the photorealistic compass and rotate the whole layer around the compass hub.
