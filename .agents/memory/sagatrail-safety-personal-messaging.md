---
name: Safety link personal messaging
description: Product decision for sending safety links from the hiker’s own WhatsApp or SMS identity.
---

Safety links intended to come from the hiker personally must open a prefilled WhatsApp chat or SMS addressed to the saved emergency contact. The hiker completes delivery with the platform-required Send tap; do not replace this with a generic share sheet or claim it is automatic.

**Why:** The user chose the personal sender identity over a paid SagaTrail business sender. iOS and WhatsApp do not allow silent sending from a user’s personal account.

**How to apply:** After a Watch-started check-in creates its live link, show direct WhatsApp and SMS actions on the iPhone. The Watch should tell the hiker to finish sending on the phone. Fully automatic delivery requires a separate server-side business provider and is a different product choice.
